WARNING: ServiceMonitor is enabled but networkPolicy.adminIngress is EMPTY.
  The NetworkPolicy omits the admin port rule when adminIngress is empty, so
  Prometheus cannot reach /metrics on port {{ .Values.statuslist.service.adminPort }}.
  Add at least one source selector to statuslist.networkPolicy.adminIngress
  (e.g. the Prometheus Operator pod/namespace selector) to unblock scraping.
